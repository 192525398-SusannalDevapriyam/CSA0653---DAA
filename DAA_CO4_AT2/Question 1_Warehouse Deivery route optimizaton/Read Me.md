\# Warehouse Delivery Route Optimization using Greedy Strategy



\## Aim



To optimize delivery routes among warehouses using a Greedy Strategy and Minimum Spanning Tree.



\## Problem Statement



A logistics company needs to connect multiple warehouses while minimizing transportation cost.



\## Algorithm Used



Prim's Algorithm



\## Concepts Used



\- Greedy Strategy

\- Minimum Spanning Tree (MST)

\- Graph Representation



\## Input Graph



9 2 4



2 7 8



8 1 4



\## Output



Total Weight of MST = 3



\## Time Complexity



O(V²)



\## Space Complexity



O(V)



\## Conclusion



Prim's Algorithm successfully finds the minimum-cost network connecting all warehouses. The greedy approach ensures efficient route selection and cost optimization.
